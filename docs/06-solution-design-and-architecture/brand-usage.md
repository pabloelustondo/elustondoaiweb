# Elustondo.ai — Initial Brand Usage

## Canonical identity

- Public brand: **Elustondo.ai**
- Legal corporation name: **Elustondo Systems Consulting Inc.**
- Brand mark: the Elustondo shield
- No `www`, `https://`, or literal `™` symbol in the standard logo.

## Logo rules

- Keep the capital **E** in `Elustondo.ai`.
- Keep `.ai` in the same forest-green colour as the rest of the wordmark.
- Keep the shield to the left of the wordmark.
- Use the short logo for articles, the website, social profiles, and compact placements.
- Use the long logo when the legal corporation needs to be visible.
- Do not redraw, regenerate, recolour, distort, shadow, or add effects to the shield.

## Shield construction

- Blue field: `#0844A3`
- Gold outline and twelve five-point stars: `#D7A742`
- Central six-point star: `#FFFDF7`
- Wordmark forest green: `#103622`
- Secondary legal-name text: `#58655F`
- Warm ivory payment-image background: `#FBFAF6`

## PayPal image

Use `Elustondo.ai-PayPal-Consulting-Hour.png` for the PayPal product/service image. PayPal itself displays the price and item details, so the image intentionally contains no price, clock, circuitry, or generic slogan.
